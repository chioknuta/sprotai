How to play

Each sprat faces one way. Click a sprat to let it swim out of the tin — it only escapes if every cell ahead of it is empty.

Bump into another fish and it stays put, and you take a bump. Empty the tin with zero bumps for three stars.

You can never get stuck: freeing a fish only ever clears the way for others.
