How to play

Tap a sprat to lift it out. It rises straight up, so every cell above it must already be clear.

Each rescue drains oil equal to the sprat's own length — a long sprat takes more oil with it. The ticks on the left wall show the drop.

Any sprat left poking above the oil line dries out and locks in place: lost, and in the way of everyone beneath it.

A perfect rescue order always exists. Save the whole tin.
